Transformation is not noise added to a stable thing. Often transformation is the only way to discover which parts of a system are stable enough to deserve explanation.

Scaling, re-encoding, abstraction, and intervention all test a different kind of persistence. Some systems preserve topology but not metric detail. Others preserve causal ordering while changing almost everything perceptible on the surface.

## Method

I tend to trust ideas that remain legible under more than one mode of observation. If a claim disappears when the coordinate system changes, it may have been a property of the description rather than of the system.
