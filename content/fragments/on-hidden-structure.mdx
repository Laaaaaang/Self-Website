What is visible is not therefore fundamental. Surface regularities matter, but they are often secondary effects of relations that are easier to obscure than to see.

In research, this means I am less interested in a particular representation than in what survives translation between representations. A graph, an equation, a simulation trace, and a learned latent space may all be partial views of the same underlying organization.

## Working Assumption

If a phenomenon remains stable when coordinates, scales, or descriptive languages change, that stability is a clue. It suggests that the phenomenon is not merely stylistic. It suggests structure.

Photography, in this sense, becomes compatible with mathematical work. A frame can register alignment, interval, tension, or recurrence before those relations are formalized elsewhere.
