Emergence is sometimes described too mysteriously. In practice, it can be approached as a question about explanatory economy.

At what level of description do relations become clearer than raw enumeration? When does a compressed account explain more than a detailed one? Those moments are not refusals of mechanism. They are indications that mechanism has reorganized itself into a more legible scale.

## Observation

The interesting difficulty is that emergence depends on viewpoint without becoming arbitrary. A useful higher-level description is still constrained by what the lower-level system can actually do.
