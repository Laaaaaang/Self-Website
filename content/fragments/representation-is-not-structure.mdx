Representations are selective. They privilege some variables, suppress others, and make certain transformations easy while making others nearly impossible to express.

This is why good performance within one representational frame is not enough. A model can predict well and still misunderstand the object it predicts if it binds itself to artifacts of encoding.

## Practical Consequence

The question is not only whether a representation works, but what kind of change it survives. When the same system is seen as topology, trajectory, or geometry, which conclusions remain valid? Those conclusions are usually closer to structure than the representational surface itself.
